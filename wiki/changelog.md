# Changelog

The canonical changelog lives at the repository root and is the single
source of truth — `scripts/cut_release.py` reads it when cutting a
release:

- [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/elenanight/windfall/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

Do not duplicate entries here. Add new items under `## [Unreleased]` in
the root file; they get promoted into the versioned section on release.
